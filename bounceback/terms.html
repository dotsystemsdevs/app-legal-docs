Description

Bounceback is a habit tracker that rewards coming back after a missed day. Your habits and history are stored locally on your device.

Bounceback Pro

Bounceback is free to use with up to three active habits. Bounceback Pro adds unlimited habits, more reminders per habit and insights.

  • What is offered: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, and a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in your local currency in the app before you buy, and are set by the App Store.
  • Free trial: the yearly subscription starts with a free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial, and the app reminds you before it ends. Unless you cancel before it ends, the subscription begins and the first payment is taken at that point.
  • Renewal: subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until cancelled. Lifetime is a single payment and never renews.
  • Cancelling: manage or cancel at any time in your Apple ID subscription settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not end the period you have already paid for.
  • Payment and refunds: payment is taken by Apple, not by us. We never see your card details. Refunds are handled under Apple’s policy, and requests go to Apple directly.
  • Your data either way: habits you already have are never locked. Without Pro they stay readable, loggable and editable; only adding habits beyond the free limit needs Pro.
