Bounceback – Habit Tracker

Need help?

For questions, feedback or bug reports, email us. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

  • How do I log a day? Tap a habit’s box once for the Minimum (half full), again for Full, and a third time to clear it.
  • How do I log yesterday? Hold a habit’s card on Today. The last days you can still change appear, each with its own box.
  • What is the bounce window? The number of planned days you have to come back after a miss before your streak starts over. Change it in Settings.
  • Going away or unwell? Settings → Freeze everything. Days pass without counting against you until you unfreeze.
  • Moving to a new phone? Settings → Export backup on the old phone, then Import backup on the new one. There is no account, so the backup file is how your history moves.
  • Restoring Pro: open the Pro screen and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
