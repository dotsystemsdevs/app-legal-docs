Privacy Policy

Crest – PR Tracker

Last updated: September 23, 2026

The short version

Crest has no account and no sign-up. Your lifts never leave your phone. The only thing that is sent is an anonymous count of which parts of the app get used, and you can switch that off in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Everything Crest knows about you is stored on your device and nowhere else:

  • Your lifts: the exercises you track, the weights, the reps and the dates
  • Your records: personal bests, estimated one rep maxes, ranks and history
  • Your profile: bodyweight and units, used to work out your rank
  • Settings: appearance, colours and preferences

We cannot see any of it. Delete the app and it is gone with it.

Photos

You can put one of your own photos behind a card you share. Crest holds a reference to that picture only while the card is on screen, and only long enough to draw it. The photo is never copied off your device and never uploaded.

Sharing a card

When you share a card, iOS hands the image to whichever app you pick. What happens to it after that is covered by that app’s own privacy policy, not ours.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to count how the app is used in aggregate. It tells us whether people get past the first lift, whether they come back, and which parts are worth building on.

What is sent: the app was opened, one “active today” event per day, a lift was logged (whether it was the first, whether it set a record, whether the exercise is a bodyweight one), a PR suggestion was shown, the rank changed, the strength profile was opened, a card was shared, and a backup was exported.

What is never sent: exercise names you typed, weights, reps, dates, notes, your bodyweight, your name or your email. We never have any of it.

No identifier is stored. The analytics id lives in memory for one launch and is never written to your phone, no profile is kept behind the events, and your IP address is discarded. Which means we can count how many people did something, and can never tell that two of those events were you.

Settings → Anonymous usage stats turns it off completely.

Subscriptions

Crest Pro is bought through Apple. We never see your name, your email or your card – Apple handles the payment and tells us only whether a subscription is active.

We use RevenueCat to receive that answer. They are given an anonymous identifier for the install and the details of the purchase itself (which plan, whether a trial is running, when it renews). They are not given your lifts, your bodyweight or anything you typed, and the identifier is not tied to you by name or email.

If you never subscribe, nothing is sent to them at all.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no email addresses, no passwords
  • No advertising identifiers, no crash reporting tied to you
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No access to your payment details – Apple keeps those

Children

Crest is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If Crest ever starts collecting anything, it will say so here first and in the app.
