For questions, feedback or bug reports, email us. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

  • How do I log a lift? Tap Log, pick the exercise, type the weight and the reps. That is the whole flow. There is no workout to start and none to finish.
  • Where does “ready for a PR” come from? A set at a lower weight for more reps says what your one rep max is worth today. When that estimate passes your real best, Crest says you are ready and suggests the next step up.
  • What are the plate numbers under it? What to load on each side of the bar to reach the suggested weight, counting the bar itself.
  • How is my rank worked out? Your lifts against your bodyweight, averaged over your main lifts, placed on a ladder from Bronze I to Elite. Keep your bodyweight up to date in settings or the rank drifts.
  • Bodyweight exercises? Chin-ups and dips are logged as weight added on top of you, so “+30 kg” means a belt with 30 kg.
  • An exercise is missing. Type its name in the search field when you log and it is added.
  • Moving to a new phone? There is no account, so your history lives on the device. Use the export in settings before you switch.
