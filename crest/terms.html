What Crest is

Crest records the weights you lift and works out what they are worth: estimated maxes, personal bests, a rank and a suggestion for when to try a new record. It is a record keeper and a calculator.

What Crest is not

Crest is not a coach and not medical advice. The suggested weights are arithmetic on the numbers you typed, not a judgement of whether you are recovered, warmed up, injured or ready. Lifting heavy carries a risk of injury. You decide what to attempt, and you do so at your own risk. If you have any doubt, talk to a doctor or a qualified coach.

Your data

Your lifts stay on your device. We hold no copy and cannot restore anything for you. Back up your phone, or use the export in settings, if the history matters to you.

Accuracy

Estimated maxes come from published formulas and are estimates, not measurements. Ranks are a comparison against bodyweight, not a certificate of anything.
